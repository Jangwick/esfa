2 · Shop

What this shows: the storefront copies its customers, sellers and orders into SFA, then asks SFA to take the payment. Every call carries the token; payments also carry a one-time Idempotency-Key so a retry never charges twice.

New customer

PUT /integrations/customers/{id}

New seller

PUT /integrations/sellers/{id}

Place an order

PUT /integrations/orders/{id} with every amount in integer centavos.

Items

Checkout (pay)

POST /integrations/payments + Idempotency-Key

Sandbox rules: a total ending in .99 is declined, .98 stays pending until the gateway reports (tab 4), above ₱50,000 waits for a Finance risk review, anything else is approved and captured. Cash on delivery stays pending until the parcel is delivered.

Cancel an order

POST /integrations/orders/{id}/cancel. SFA refuses once money was captured; that needs a refund instead.

Shipping quote

POST /integrations/quotes using PSGC city codes. Shipments themselves are booked in SFA by the Logistics Coordinator.

Check a status in SFA

GET /integrations/{payments|refunds|shipments}/{reference}

      3 · Customer support

      What this shows: a customer asks for help on the storefront and the ticket lands in SFA's support queue. When an SFA agent replies or changes the status, SFA tells the storefront with a signed webhook.

      Customer asks for help

      POST /integrations/tickets + Idempotency-Key

      Customer replies

      POST /integrations/tickets/{reference}/messages

      Refresh a ticket

      GET /integrations/tickets/{reference} returns public messages only; internal agent notes never leave SFA.

      Customer rates the help

      POST /integrations/tickets/{reference}/csat. SFA accepts it only after the agent resolves the ticket.

      Customer requests a refund

      POST /integrations/refunds + Idempotency-Key. Finance approves or rejects it in SFA.

          4 · Payment gateway

          What this shows: the bank or e-wallet tells SFA what happened to a payment. It uses no token: SFA trusts the message only because it is signed with a shared secret and is less than five minutes old.

          Send a gateway event

          POST /webhooks/payments/sandbox signed with X-SFA-Signature

          Tip: pay an order whose total ends in .98 (tab 2); it stays pending until you send Captured here.

            5 · Courier

            What this shows: the delivery company pushes parcel updates to SFA with a signed webhook. SFA updates the shipment and forwards the news to the storefront.

            Send a delivery update

            POST /webhooks/couriers/{code} signed with X-SFA-Signature

            SFA enforces the delivery order (pending → ready for pickup → picked up → in transit → out for delivery) and rejects skipped steps.

            Shipments

            Booking a shipment through the API needs SFA's internal warehouse and address IDs, which the integration API does not expose, so the Logistics Coordinator books shipments in SFA (Logistics & Locations → Shipments). This list fills from shipment.created webhooks and courier replies. Seeded tracking numbers DEMO-TRK-0001…0015 work too.
