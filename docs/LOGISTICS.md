# Logistics contracts and source data

The location development subset uses official PSA PSGC codes, verified on 9 October 2026 from [the NCR listing](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/provinces/1300000000) and [the City of Las Piñas barangay listing](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/barangays/1380200000). NCR does not have provinces; its city records and addresses therefore have a nullable province. No district is invented as a province. Courier names, rates and zones labeled Demo are synthetic development examples.

`POST /locations/import` accepts the PSA publication saved as CSV with `10-digit PSGC`, `Name`, and `Geographic Level` headers, or normalized `code,name,level` headers. Supported geographic levels are Reg/Region, Prov/Province, City, Mun/Municipality, and Bgy/Barangay. Preserve ten digit codes as text. Optional `region_code,province_code,city_code` columns override parents; otherwise official code prefixes resolve the parent from imported records. District and submunicipality rows are skipped, because they are not provinces. The whole import is atomic and repeated imports upsert by code.

Quotes accept `origin_city_id` or `origin_code`, `destination_city_id` or `destination_code`, integer `weight_grams`, `dimensions:{length_cm,width_cm,height_cm}` in centimeters with up to two decimal places, and integer `cod_amount` centavos. Volumetric grams round upward; fees use integer centavos and half-up COD basis point rounding. All matching active rates are returned, cheapest first, with `rate_id`, component fees, chargeable weight and business-time-zone ETA dates. The first (cheapest) quote has `recommended: true`.

Creating a shipment requires order/seller IDs for staff or external IDs scoped to the integration client's `system_code`, origin warehouse, destination address, weight, dimensions, COD amount and optional rate ID/tracking number. Courier and service are optional but sent together: without them, create books the recommended quote and an edit keeps the current courier and service. The server recomputes the shipping fee and promises. One shipment is permitted per order and seller, including cancelled shipments. Configuration records referenced by shipments or rates cannot be deleted and can be deactivated instead.

The Awaiting booking list (`GET /logistics/awaiting-booking`, `create shipments`) shows order and seller pairs with no shipment yet, for orders that are not cancelled and have a captured payment or a pending COD payment not held for risk review. Each row suggests the origin (the seller's active pickup point, else the first active hub), the COD amount (only for a single-seller order) and the customer's last delivery address. The dashboard counts them as `awaiting_booking`.

On the third failed attempt, a returning event is appended automatically. Delivered status requires a recipient, proof photo and cash confirmation for COD. Photo upload atomically delivers the shipment. A single order's COD payment is captured only when every COD shipment is delivered with cash collected and their COD amounts total the payment exactly. This prevents the first parcel of a split seller order from capturing cash that has not been collected.

With `logistics_auto_ticket` on (the default), each failed attempt opens a `delivery` ticket for the customer (channel `system`) or notes the shipment's open ticket; the third attempt makes it high priority and a delivery adds a note. `GET /logistics/shipments?overdue=1` lists undelivered shipments past their last promised day in the business timezone (`summary.overdue` counts them), and scheduled work sends staff who edit shipments one digest of newly overdue shipments and of shipments with no event for `logistics_stuck_hours` (default 48, 0 = off).

Courier webhooks use `X-SFA-Signature: t=<unix>,v1=<HMAC-SHA256(timestamp.raw-body)>`, a five-minute timestamp tolerance, and `id,tracking_number,status` in the JSON body. Optional location/note/occurred_at populate the timeline. Event IDs are unique per courier and duplicate deliveries are ignored. This generic signed contract does not claim compatibility with a real courier API. Delivered webhooks are rejected when required proof is missing. With `logistics_courier_pod` on (default off), a delivered webhook for a prepaid parcel that is out for delivery may carry `recipient_name` and `proof_photo` (raw base64 PNG, JPG or WEBP up to 2 MB) as its proof; COD parcels always need staff proof with cash collection. A skipped forward step is rejected (422) unless `logistics_courier_fill_gaps` is on (default off): then the skipped steps are recorded first, noted as inferred, and delivered is never inferred. Every rejected courier event notifies the staff who edit shipments once per event id (`courier_event_rejected`).

Picked up requires a tracking number. When a COD parcel is returned to the sender or cancelled, and no other COD parcel of the order is still open and every seller has a shipment, the pending COD payment is cancelled; a returned or cancelled parcel of a prepaid order notifies the staff who edit payments (`parcel_returned`). Cancelling an order cancels its pending and ready-for-pickup shipments, and is refused (422) while a shipment is with the courier or delivered.
